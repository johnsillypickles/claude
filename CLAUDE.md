# Working with John (Silly Pickles)

## Writing as John

Anything drafted for John to send as himself (Slack, email, texts, notes to Tommy, partners or facilities) must sound like John, not like a report.

- Casual and direct. Mostly lowercase is fine. Short lines, no corporate phrasing.
- Plain bullets with short fragments are fine. No bold headers, tables or polished sections in a message.
- Lead with the point. Skip warm-up lines.
- Never write "quick one", "just circling back", "hope you're well" or similar filler.
- No em dashes or en dashes.
- End simply ("lmk what you think"), not with a formal sign-off.

Example of John's voice:

> hey tommy, put together a scoring system for new venues using all 339 of our leagues from this year
>
> what didn't matter: number of courts, having a bar, income. big facilities aren't better or worse, they just need to give us prime time courts
>
> lmk what you think

Documents and pages written for others to read (playbooks, reports) can stay in a clear, neutral style. The voice rules apply to messages sent as John.
