# Competitor Research: Volo Sports and Honcho Pickleball

Prepared for: Silly Pickles (sillypickles.com)
Research date: 2026-09-30

## Method and limitations (read first)

- Research was done with web search only. Direct page fetches (sillypickles.com, volosports.com, honchopickleball.com, Instagram, TikTok, Meta Ad Library) were blocked by the research environment's network proxy. Every fact below comes from search engine result snippets and the pages they summarize, so treat it as secondhand until someone checks the live page.
- Follower counts come from search snippets of profile pages. The date each snippet was captured is **unknown**. They are labeled "snippet, capture date unknown" and should be treated as approximate. Re-check live before quoting.
- No post-level engagement metrics (likes, views, comments) could be verified. Where this report says a format "likely works," that is analysis, not measured data.
- Meta Ad Library, TikTok Creative Center and similar tools could not be queried. Paid signals are inferred from job postings and creator disclosures.

## 0. Who Silly Pickles is (verified context)

- Silly Pickles describes itself as "home to the world's funnest LGBTQ-owned pickleball leagues," LGBTQ+ founded and inclusive, starting in Chicago. Sources: [About Silly Pickles](https://sillypickles.com/pages/about-silly-pickles), [Chicago leagues page](https://sillypickles.com/pages/pickleball-leagues-chicago-illinois).
- Format: 8-week leagues, "Dynamic Ladder Format," most players sign up solo and are matched for them, all levels welcome. Source: [Visit Kingston event listing](https://www.visitkingston.ca/events/lets-get-silly-kingston-pickleball-league/).
- Footprint seen in search results: Chicago, Atlanta, San Francisco, Columbus, Las Vegas, Thornton CO, Wichita KS, Kingston ON, Calgary AB. The site reportedly claims 50+ locations (not independently verified). Sources: [Atlanta collection](https://sillypickles.com/collections/atlanta-georgia), [San Francisco collection](https://sillypickles.com/collections/san-francisco-california), [Las Vegas Pride calendar](https://lasvegaspride.org/event/silly-pickles-pickleball-league/2025-11-09/), [LGBTQ Colorado](https://lgbtqcolorado.org/events/2025-04-27), [Sweatpals Wichita](https://sweatpals.com/event/wichita-pickleball-league-at-tapnpaddles-sundays-46pm-starts-jan-11/), [Sport Calgary](https://sportcalgary.ca/event/silly-pickles-pickleball-at-mega-courts/2026-06-14/).
- Prices seen in listings: Kingston $118, Calgary $148, Thornton $165 per league (currency per listing, likely CAD for Canadian cities).
- Instagram handle: @sillypickles (from search snippet).

So the competitive set is: **social adult pickleball leagues, solo sign-up, ladder play, community-first.** Both named competitors are real overlaps.

## 2. Volo Sports

### Business snapshot
- Adult (21+) social sports company founded in Baltimore (2010 or 2011 depending on source) by founder and CEO Giovanni Marcantoni. Leagues, pickups, drop-ins, tournaments, social events, corporate programs, plus volunteer opportunities through the **Volo Kids Foundation** (free youth sports). Sources: [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/volosports), [Crunchbase: Marcantoni](https://www.crunchbase.com/person/giovanni-marcantoni-6dfc), [Hoodline](https://hoodline.com/2026/07/serpentine-pavilion-turns-into-downtown-san-jose-s-new-pickleball-party-yard/).
- Multi-sport: soccer, kickball, volleyball, softball, flag football, basketball, dodgeball, cornhole, futsal, pickleball. Source: [Volo San Diego](https://www.volosports.com/san-diego).
- Scale and capital:
  - About $37.7M total funding reported (aggregator figure). Source: [PitchBook profile](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/454451-41) (via search snippet).
  - $21M growth investment from Bluestone Equity Partners in November 2024. Source: [Bluestone](https://bluestoneequity.com/volo-sports-acquires-zogsports-to-create-nations-largest-and-most-popular-social-recreational-sports-network/).
  - Acquired ZogSports (120K+ players) on June 26, 2025, bringing Volo to 11 major metros and 500K+ players. Sources: [Business Wire](https://secure.businesswire.com/news/home/20250626808883/en/Volo-Sports-Acquires-ZogSports-to-Create-Nations-Largest-and-Most-Popular-Social-Recreational-Sports-Network), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-06-26/bluestone-backed-sports-platform-volo-acquires-rival-zogsports).
  - Reported 750,000+ player registrations across 4,500+ leagues "so far in 2025." Source: [PitchBook news](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/volo-sports-wants-to-dominate-your-social-life) (via snippet).
- Markets seen: NYC metro, Boston, DC, Baltimore, Philadelphia, Chicago, Denver, Miami, LA, San Diego, San Francisco, San Jose, Charleston, Morristown NJ. Sources: city pages on volosports.com, e.g. [Philadelphia pickleball](https://www.volosports.com/philadelphia/pickleball).
- **Owned venues ("Club Volo")**: Assembly Row, Somerville MA, and San Jose (Serpentine Pavilion, opened 2026 with six outdoor pickleball courts, sand volleyball, futsal; launch had free play, live music, and a pickup game with Mayor Matt Mahan). Sources: [Assembly Row](https://assemblyrow.com/explore/club-volo/), [Hoodline](https://hoodline.com/2026/07/serpentine-pavilion-turns-into-downtown-san-jose-s-new-pickleball-party-yard/).
- Positioning: "make friends through sports," low pressure, social first, not about athletic skill.

### Pricing (public)
- Pickleball league example: San Francisco from $138.41 per player for a 7-week session. Source: [Volo discover SF](https://www.volosports.com/discover/san-francisco?category=leagues&sports=pickleball&includeFees=true).
- **Volo Pass** membership: $35/month monthly (3-month minimum); annual plan reported as "just under $30/month" in one snippet and "$25/month" in another (conflicting, check live). Includes unlimited standard pickups and league drop-ins in every city, league discounts, early registration, quarterly "kickback" rewards based on activity, and a reported $100 signing bonus for annual members. Pickleball daily play "as low as $2 a session" with the Pass. Source: [Volo Pass](https://www.volosports.com/volo-pass).

### Content strategy
Evidence from Volo's own social job posting (Social Content Creator / Junior Designer, Digital Designer, Social): content should capture "the energy of real people playing sports together," with graphics, trend and meme identification, and on-site capture from leagues and events across Instagram, TikTok and YouTube. Source: [TeamWork Online](https://www.teamworkonline.com/youth-sports-jobs/volo-sports-jobs/volo-sports-jobs/digital-designer-social-2166495).

Inferred pillars (from job posting plus TikTok search results):
1. Real players on real game nights (UGC style, on-site capture)
2. Trend and meme content
3. "Come solo, make friends" first-timer stories
4. Event and themed-night promos (mixers, Pride events, festivals)
5. Give-back content (Volo Kids Foundation)

Tone: upbeat, party-social, "adult recess." Posting cadence: **unverified**.

### What is working (with examples)
- **Creator partnerships with discount codes (#volopartner).** Local lifestyle and food creators post "I tried Volo" videos with codes. Examples:
  - @thesnacksensei (SF): "KICKBALL & FLIPCUP ON A WEDNESDAY!", code SENSEI15 for $15 off first league, new players only, tagged #volopartner. [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@thesnacksensei/video/7627217182279224590)
  - @andrewtourssf (SF): first-timer story, "I am no way an athlete," joined pickup soccer, bought Volo Pass, mentions Volo Kids Foundation, #volovibes. [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@andrewtourssf/video/7330710890439773470)
  - Other codes seen in search results: CHLOE25, ALLISON15. [TikTok discover: Volo Sports Code](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/volo-sports-code)
  - Why it likely works: the creator is a non-athlete, which removes intimidation; city-specific; code gives trackable attribution and urgency (expiry date). Engagement numbers unverified.
- **Organic review and discovery content.** TikTok has discover pages such as "Volo Sports NYC review," "Volo Sports Boston," "Volo Sports dodgeball," which suggests steady user-made content. [NYC review](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/volo-sports-nyc-review)
- **Reality TV adjacency.** A search snippet notes Summer House cast member West Wilson featured with Volo. Details unverified.
- **Themed social events** that give people a reason to post: "Dink n Date" pickleball mixer and "Kings/Queens of the Court" volleyball mixer at Club Volo, a "Sweat Social Festival" with HYROX, LGBTQ Draft Beach Volleyball Tournament and "Dinkin' with Dad" pickleball tournament during Pride/Father's Day season. Sources: [Boston Calendar: Dink n Date](https://www.thebostoncalendar.com/events/must-register-on-volo-website-dink-n-date-pickleball-mixer-club-volo), [Boston Calendar: Sweat Social](https://www.thebostoncalendar.com/events/volo-s-sweat-social-festival-hyrox), [Baltimore Fishbowl](https://baltimorefishbowl.com/?p=188684).
- **Civic launch moments** (San Jose mayor pickup game) that earn local press.

### What seems weak or missing
- Owned-channel following looks small relative to player count; it seems to lean on creators and city accounts.
- Multi-sport brand: pickleball is one of ~10 sports, so there is no deep pickleball identity, no skill ratings or pickleball-specific culture.
- Scale, PE ownership and roll-up acquisitions can feel corporate; the "local crew" feeling is harder to sell.
- 21+ and bar-league framing may not suit sober-curious or older players.
- LGBTQ+ programming appears seasonal (Pride Month events) rather than a year-round identity.

## 3. Honcho Pickleball League

### Business snapshot
- Founded late 2023 by Aaron Sunstrum (Founder and CEO), a former consulting firm partner and longtime beach volleyball league player. Started from backyard games in Houston; second season had 150 players by word of mouth. Sources: [Pickleball Union](https://pickleballunion.com/pickleballs-head-honcho-honcho-pickleball-league-embraces-nationwide-pickleball-community/), [Aaron Sunstrum LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/aaron-sunstrum-a7a963131/).
- Pitch: "America's premier amateur pickleball league," "largest amateur PB league and community." Social plus **skill-based** play.
- Products:
  - **Rotating-Partner Ladder League**: sign up solo, placed in a foursome each week, three games with three partner pairings. This is the most direct overlap with Silly Pickles.
  - **Same-Partner Doubles League**: sign up as a team, placed by division.
  - **Honcho Amateur Tour Powered by JOOLA**: 4-stop pilot in 2025 (reported 9.3/10 average player satisfaction), expanding to **8 "festival-style" weekends in 2026**. Division winners get $150 cash plus JOOLA paddles; Open division takes 30% of the division-fee pot. Sources: [JOOLA 2026 announcement](https://joola.com/blogs/updates/the-honcho-amateur-tour-powered-by-joola-returns-expanding-to-eight-festival-style-weekends-in-2026), [JOOLA partnership (May 15, 2025)](https://joola.com/blogs/updates/honcho-pr).
- Footprint: early seasons in Ann Arbor, Atlanta, Charlotte, Chicago, Miami, Phoenix, then Austin, Dallas, LA. Now "70+ locations" per press, "80+ cities" per Instagram bio, plus Canada. Figures differ by source and date. Sources: [PicklrLab](https://picklrlab.com/news/honcho-pickleball-leagues-now-at-70-locations-nationwide), [Honcho Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/honchopickleball/).
- Model: partners with existing facilities (examples: Rally Club, Pickleball America, Fairgrounds Capilano, East County Pickleball Courts via PlayByPoint). Sources: [Rally Club](https://www.rallyclubpickleball.com/honcho), [Pickleball America partner page](https://honchopickleball.com/partner/pickleball-america/).
- Partnerships: **DUPR** (ratings; league unified under DUPR ratings) and **JOOLA** (official partner, tour presenting sponsor). Sources: [DUPR](https://dupr.com/post/honcho-pickleball-league-partners-with-dupr-to-drive-growth-unify-league-with-global-rating-system), [JOOLA](https://joola.com/blogs/blog/joola-partners-with-honcho-pickleball).
- Funding: none found in search. Unverified.

Champion prizes described as "$600+." Sources: [Register page](https://honchopickleball.com/register/), [Honcho home](https://honchopickleball.com/).

### Content strategy (inferred, owned posts not viewable)
Likely pillars based on bio, press and partner content:
1. Champions and prize announcements (trophies, $600+ prize packages)
2. Tour weekend recaps with "music festival" energy
3. Partner and sponsor content (JOOLA paddles, DUPR ratings)

Tone: competitive-aspirational, "premier," "largest," "Head Honcho" status language. Posting cadence: unverified.

### What is working
- **Sponsor-backed tour as a content engine.** Eight festival weekends in 2026 generate repeatable recap content, sponsor co-posts (JOOLA's own blog and channels amplify it), and a prize story. Satisfaction claim (9.3/10) is used in PR.
- **DUPR integration** gives players a rating they care about and a reason to keep playing and posting progress.
- **Partner-facility model** lets Honcho appear on venue sites and booking apps (PlayByPoint listings), which is free distribution.
- **Clear, tangible value stack** at registration (courts booked, tee, social, prizes), which reduces price objections.
- Specific high-engagement posts: **not verifiable** from this environment.

### What seems weak or missing
- No TikTok or short-form presence found.
- Positioning leans competitive and "premier," which may put off true beginners and people who care more about the social side.
- Pricing isn't shown up front on national pages.
- Community identity is generic (no strong culture or affinity group); brand is about scale and prizes.
- Rapid venue growth risks uneven local experience; that's a common risk for asset-light league operators (inference, not verified).

## 5. Key takeaways for Silly Pickles

1. **Own the identity neither competitor has.** Volo's LGBTQ+ content is seasonal and Honcho's brand is about scale and prizes. Silly Pickles can be the year-round queer and allies pickleball community with a real point of view, and every piece of content should make that clear.
2. **Win short-form video, especially TikTok.** Honcho has no visible TikTok and Volo sits at about 20K. Funny, "silly" game-night reels (bad shots, costume nights, first-timer reactions, partner-rotation chaos) fit the brand name and a gap in the market.
3. **Copy Volo's creator-code playbook at local scale.** Recruit local LGBTQ+ and lifestyle creators in each city for "I'm not an athlete but I tried Silly Pickles" videos, each with a trackable first-league code and an expiry date.
4. **Build an ambassador program like Honcho's, with a twist.** Name a "Head Pickle" per city: on-site hosts who welcome solo players, shoot content and bring in local queer networks. Reward them with free leagues and merch.
5. **Keep pricing transparent and stack the value.** Honcho hides prices but lists what's included; Volo shows prices. Do both: a clear price, plus a checklist of what's included (courts, matchmaking, socials, tee if any).
6. **Tie into Pride and community calendars all year.** Silly Pickles already shows up on Pride and LGBTQ community event calendars (Las Vegas Pride, LGBTQ Colorado). Push harder with Pride orgs, queer bars and sports clubs, where Volo only shows up in June.
7. **Create signature themed events that people want to post.** Volo's "Dink n Date" mixer and Honcho's festival weekends show that events drive content. Examples to consider: drag-hosted finals, "Pickle Prom," singles mixers, and a city-vs-city Silly Cup.
8. **Consider a light membership or loyalty loop.** Volo Pass shows that subscriptions and kickbacks drive repeat play. A simple multi-season discount or "play 3 seasons, get a perk" could raise retention without Volo's overhead.
9. **Stay beginner-first, since Honcho leans competitive.** Honcho's DUPR and prize focus leaves room for "no ratings anxiety, just fun" messaging. Optionally offer DUPR-tracked divisions later for players who want to compete.
10. **Verify before acting.** Re-check follower counts, Honcho's TikTok status, and both companies' live ads in the Meta Ad Library (facebook.com/ads/library) from a normal browser. None of that could be confirmed from this research environment.
