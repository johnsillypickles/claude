# 50 Metros to Partner In (pulled 10/7/26)

Two lists of 25:

- **A. Metros where we sell out over and over.** Add a second or third facility partner here. Courts at the current facility are often maxed.
- **B. Metros where we have no league.** These are new facility partners.

## Sources

| Signal | Source |
|---|---|
| Fall 2026 sellouts, waitlists, court request outcomes | "More Courts Requests" sheet, Request Tracker tab (as of 10/6/26) |
| Waitlist count by season | Same sheet, Stoq tab (Shopify app). This shows how often a market sells out |
| Current footprint and past leagues | Shopify league products (Spring 2025 through Winter 2027) |
| Demand in metros with no league | Shopify online store sessions by city, last 180 days |

## Caveats

- **"Consistently" is based on limited history.** Stoq waitlists only go back to about Fall 2025, and they have no Summer 2026 rows. Shopify inventory doesn't reliably show sellouts because ending inventory gets bumped after the fact, so I didn't use it.
- **Waitlist totals add up all seasons.** One person can be counted in more than one season.
- **Session city comes from IP lookup, so it's noisy.** I left out cities that look like data centers, such as Council Bluffs IA (3,704), Ashburn VA, Boardman OR and Prineville OR. That they are data centers is my inference.
- **List B sessions are mostly unpaid demand.** Our ads target metros where we already run leagues, so traffic from other cities mostly arrives on its own (inference).
