      How leagues at each score filled

      Each dot is one 2026 league, placed by its venue's score. Filled dots filled at least 80% of their spots. The line marks this site. Fill is players divided by spots offered, so big and small facilities are compared fairly.

      What the score predicts. Whether a league here fills at least 80% of its spots, and how much Phase 1 ad spend it took to get there. Most leagues reach 30 players, but often with help: 132 of 339 leagues ran Phase 1 ads ($281,000 total), and leagues at venues scoring under 35 needed about $16 of Phase 1 spend per spot vs $7 to $8 elsewhere. Free and 50%-off launch codes added only 2 to 16 players per league. Facility size does not change fill, so a big facility is not penalized.

      How the score works. Each factor is ranked against our 2026 venues (0 to 100), multiplied by its weight and summed. Weights: young adults aged 25 to 39 within 7 miles per Silly Pickles league already there 25%, few kids under 5 25%, pickleball search interest 20%, young adults' share of the population 15%, people moving in from out of state 10%, density 5%. Onsite bar and court count are shown as checks because neither predicted fill.

      Pricing. Price per player-hour = court cost ÷ (spots per court-hour × (1 − target margin)), times 0.95 below a score of 35, 1.05 from 55 to 65 and 1.10 at 65+. With a 60% target this matches what we charged in 2026 within 2% at the median, and our 2026 prices already ran about $18.50 per hour at weaker locations and $21 at strong ones. Break-even uses the expected fill for the score. Expected Phase 1 spend is the average per spot for leagues at similar scores. Profit excludes hosts, Phase 0 ads and merch.

      Sources. Census ACS 2020 to 2024 by ZIP, measured within 7 miles of the ZIP's center as a stand-in for a 15 minute drive. Google Trends for the nearest metro. Our league tracker and Shopify sales, Winter to Fall 2026.
