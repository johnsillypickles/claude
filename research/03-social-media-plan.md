# Silly Pickles: Social Media Plan (Oct to Dec 2026)

Prepared 2026-09-30. Inputs: `01-competitor-research.md` (Volo Sports, Honcho Pickleball) and `02-social-media-audit.md` (our audit, 2025-10-01 to 2026-09-29).

## How to read this plan

- Every recommendation carries a source tag:
  - **[Comp: ...]** = from the competitor research (Volo or Honcho).
  - **[Audit: ...]** = from our own audit.
  - **[Inference]** = our reasoning, not measured data. Test before trusting.
- **The audit is paid-only.** Instagram, Facebook Organic and TikTok Organic were not connected, so we have zero organic numbers (followers, reach, saves, posting cadence). Everything we "know works" comes from ads. Organic recommendations apply paid learnings to organic and are therefore partly inference.
- **Competitor follower counts are unverified** (search snippets with unknown capture dates). Do not quote them externally until re-checked.
- **Targets are labeled "Proposed target"** with the basis stated. No organic targets are set until we have a baseline (see Section 9).

## 1. Executive summary

1. **Lead with the identity nobody else owns.** Volo's LGBTQ+ content is seasonal and Honcho's brand is about scale and prizes, yet our own top 100 Meta ads never mention that we are LGBTQ-owned. Make "queer-owned, all-levels, come solo" the center of every channel and test it in paid. [Comp: Volo Pride events seasonal] [Comp: Honcho generic identity] [Audit: LGBTQ-owned absent from top 100 copy]
2. **Judge content by signups, not likes or clicks.** Our highest-CTR hook ("psst... 20 minutes away", CTR 2.43%) had one of the worst CACs ($92.51), while "Less scrolling, more serving" (CTR 0.68%) returned ROAS up to 4.26. [Audit: CTR and ROAS decoupled]
3. **Scale what converts, cut what does not.** Scale "starting soon / All Levels Welcome" (ROAS 3.12), "Fall/Winter signups are open" (ROAS 3.08), the ladder explainer, and homepage or all-collections landing pages. Stop broad discount codes (SUMMERBALL20 ROAS 0.56), "Learn More" CTAs, single-league landing pages and the current volleyball creative. [Audit: What did work / What did not work]
4. **Win short-form video with a recurring host and local creators.** Tommy talking-head videos carry 65% of TikTok spend and the seasonal "Boo" version had the best cost per conversion ($39.27). Honcho has no TikTok found. Add a Volo-style creator code program and a Honcho-style ambassador ("Head Pickle") program per city. [Audit: Tommy 65% of TikTok spend] [Comp: Volo creator codes] [Comp: Honcho ambassadors] [Comp: Honcho no TikTok]
5. **Fix the plumbing in week 1.** Connect organic IG/FB/TikTok in Windsor.ai, fix the @sillypickles vs @sillysports split, set a Facebook vanity URL, and consolidate two affiliate apps before launching creator codes. [Audit: Gaps] [Audit: UpPromote and BixGrow both installed]

## 2. Positioning and brand voice

### Positioning statement (proposed)

> **Silly Pickles is the world's funnest pickleball league: LGBTQ-owned, all levels welcome, and built for people who show up solo and leave with a crew.**

Basis: "World's Funnest" and "All Levels Welcome" are already in our best-performing copy [Audit: "starting soon! All Levels Welcome!" ROAS 3.12]. "LGBTQ-owned" is our verified identity [Comp: Section 0] but untested in ads [Audit: LGBTQ-owned absent from top 100 copy].

### How we differ

| | Volo Sports | Honcho Pickleball | Silly Pickles (how we win) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core identity | Multi-sport "adult recess," 21+ bar-league framing [Comp: Volo weak spots] | "Premier," "largest," competitive, DUPR, prizes [Comp: Honcho tone] | Queer-owned, year-round community, pickleball-first |
| LGBTQ+ | Seasonal Pride events only [Comp: Volo LGBTQ seasonal] | None found [Comp: Side-by-side] | Core identity, every week of the year |
| Skill stance | Social, no skill culture | Skill-based, DUPR ratings | "Play at your level" ladder, no ratings anxiety [Audit: DiffSkillLevels $41.09/conv] [Comp: takeaway 9] |
| Feel | Big, PE-backed, corporate risk [Comp: Volo weak spots] | Scale and prizes | Local crew, a host who knows your name |
| Pricing | Public | Hidden, value stack listed [Comp: Honcho pricing] | Public price plus a value checklist [Comp: takeaway 5] |
| TikTok | ~20K (unverified) | Not found | Our biggest open lane |

### Voice rules

**Do:**
- Playful, self-deprecating, inclusive. Keep the proven lines: "The World's Funnest Pickleball League," "Pickleball, but make it social," "All Levels Welcome," "Athletic? Debatable. Fun? Guaranteed.," "Less scrolling, more serving," "Your social life called..." [Audit: Brand voice]
- Say "queer-owned" or "LGBTQ-owned" plainly and proudly. Allies are welcome and should be told so. [Audit: LGBTQ-owned absent] [Inference: explicit ally welcome widens reach without diluting identity]
- Speak to the solo, nervous beginner first. [Audit: "Never tried pickleball?" hook] [Comp: Volo non-athlete creators]
- Keep the cowboy and star emojis as brand signatures. [Audit: Brand voice]

**Do not:**
- Use "premier," "elite," "competitive" framing. That is Honcho's lane. [Comp: Honcho tone]
- Use random title case. Standardize on sentence case: "Pickleball, but make it social." and "Never tried pickleball? This is your sign!" [Audit: capitalization inconsistencies]
- Rotate between "funnest" and "funnest ever." Pick "funnest." [Audit: minor inconsistencies]
- Treat Pride as a June campaign. [Comp: Volo LGBTQ seasonal]

**Visual rules:** Use the theme palette (navy #201747, gold #ffb81c, orange #d86018, slate #55728f, mint #8fd6bd) and Poppins on all graphics and text overlays. [Audit: Visuals] Whether current posts follow this could not be checked, so audit the last 30 posts once access is live.

All proven copy ("World's Funnest Pickleball League") and the verified third-party listings use Silly Pickles. ### A. Come solo, leave with a crew

- **Hook:** "Never tried pickleball? This is your sign!" [Audit: CTR 2.04%, ROAS 2.76]
- **Formats:** TikTok/Reel, 20 to 40 seconds. Before (nervous solo arrival) and after (group photo at week 8).
- **Post ideas:**
  - "I showed up alone. Week 8 I had a group chat." Player POV.
  - Tommy greets a first-timer at check-in, follows them through three rotations.
  - Creator "I'm not an athlete but I tried Silly Pickles" (see Section 7). [Comp: @andrewtourssf "I am no way an athlete"]
- **CTA:** "Join solo. We'll match you." Link to /collections.

### B. Play at your level

- **Hooks:** "Less scrolling, more serving." / "Athletic? Debatable. Fun? Guaranteed." [Audit: Brand voice]
- **Formats:** Tommy talking head with on-screen text; simple animated ladder diagram. Static single image for paid tests (single image ROAS 3.34 on 1 ad, too small to trust). [Audit: format table]
- **Post ideas:**
  - "How the Dynamic Ladder works in 30 seconds."
  - "Different skill levels, same fun night" (remake of DiffSkillLevels). [Audit: DiffSkillLevels]
  - Stitch format: "Worried you're too bad at pickleball? Watch week 1 vs week 8."
  - Contrast with competitive leagues without naming them: "No ratings anxiety. Just fun." [Comp: takeaway 9]

### C. Queer joy, year-round

- **Hook ideas (new, untested):** "Pickleball, but make it queer." / "LGBTQ-owned. All levels. All welcome." [Inference: extends proven "Pickleball, but make it social"]
- **Formats:** Reels, player spotlights (carousel is fine organically for storytelling), collab posts with local LGBTQ+ orgs.
- **Post ideas:**
  - Founder story: why an LGBTQ-owned league. [Comp: About Silly Pickles]
  - "Meet your Head Pickle" city host intros.
  - National Coming Out Day (Oct 11) and Spirit Day (Oct 15) posts from players, not ads.
  - Pride org and queer bar partner shoutouts in each city. [Comp: takeaway 6]
- **Care note:** Keep awareness days (for example Transgender Day of Remembrance, Nov 20) non-promotional. [Inference]

### D. Silly game nights and themed events

- **Hook:** Seasonal host video, e.g. "Boo" Halloween version. [Audit: Boo-v2 $39.27 per conversion]
- **Formats:** On-site phone capture by Head Pickles, trending audio, meme formats. [Comp: Volo job posting, on-site capture and memes]
- **Post ideas:**
  - Costume night (Halloween week).
  - "Pickle Prom" or drag-hosted season finals. [Comp: takeaway 7]
  - "Dink n Date"-style singles mixer adapted as a queer singles night. [Comp: Volo Dink n Date]
  - Bloopers: worst shot of the week, voted in Stories.
  - Paddle reveal: "Which Silly Paddle are you?" (The OG, Check Mate, Main Squeeze, Retro Waves, What The Duck). [Audit: Section 1]
- **Later (2027):** a city-vs-city "Silly Cup" as our answer to Honcho's tour weekends. [Comp: Honcho tour content engine] [Comp: takeaway 7]

### E. Signups, seasons and paddles

- **Hooks:** "Winter signups are open!" / "[City], your league is starting soon! All Levels Welcome!" [Audit: top two hooks by ROAS]
- **Formats:** Dynamic image and video in paid; Reel plus Story countdown sticker organically.
- **Value checklist (every signup post):** 8 weeks of play, courts booked, matched for you, all levels, socials [confirm what is included per city]. Plus the public price. [Comp: Honcho value stack] [Comp: takeaway 5]
- **Paddles:** holiday gift posts in late Nov and Dec. [Inference: paddle sales were not measured in the audit]

## 7. Creator, ambassador and community program

### 7.1 Creator codes ("Silly Partners"), adapted from Volo

What Volo does: local lifestyle and food creators, often non-athletes, post "I tried Volo" videos with a first-league code and expiry, tagged #volopartner. [Comp: Volo creator codes]

Our version:
- **Who:** local LGBTQ+ and lifestyle creators in 3 pilot cities. Prefer self-described non-athletes. [Comp: @andrewtourssf, @thesnacksensei] Pick pilot cities where we already have community calendar presence (for example Las Vegas, Colorado) and Chicago. [Comp: Section 0] [Inference: existing presence lowers risk]
- **Brief:** "I'm not an athlete but I tried Silly Pickles." Film arrival solo, the ladder rotation, the social after. End on the code.
- **Code:** creator name plus amount, e.g. "NAME15." Fixed dollar, first league only, new players only, expires in 30 days. [Comp: SENSEI15 structure] Amount is a proposed figure mirroring Volo's $15; confirm margin.
- **Disclosure:** #sillypartner and platform paid-partnership labels. [Comp: #volopartner]
- **Tracking:** pick one of UpPromote or BixGrow before launch. [Audit: two affiliate apps]
- **Pay:** free league entry plus a Silly Paddle plus a per-signup fee (amount to be set by finance). [Inference]
- **Boost:** put the best-performing creator video behind paid spend as Spark Ads (TikTok) or partnership ads (Meta). [Inference]
- **Pass/fail (proposed):** CAC via code at or below $50.89, the "starting soon" hook CAC. If code CAC trends toward SUMMERBALL20 levels, stop. [Audit: $50.89 and $262.46]

### 7.2 "Head Pickle" ambassadors, adapted from Honcho

What Honcho does: on-site "community champions" at league nights, recruiting outgoing, organized players per city. [Comp: Honcho ambassadors]

Our twist: the Head Pickle is host plus content capture plus community connector. [Comp: takeaway 4]
- **Duties:** welcome solo players by name, shoot 3 short clips per league night (fun moment, first-timer, group shot), and connect with local queer bars, Pride orgs and sports clubs. [Comp: Volo on-site capture] [Comp: takeaway 6]
- **Rewards:** free league, merch, first pick on new paddles. [Comp: takeaway 4]
- **Tools:** a one-page Head Pickle handbook (Honcho uses a Notion Player Handbook) with a shot list, consent rules (ask before filming, especially players who are not out publicly), brand voice sheet and upload folder. [Comp: Honcho Player Handbook] [Inference: consent matters more for an LGBTQ+ community]
- **Pilot:** same 3 cities as creators in Oct; add cities in Nov to Dec if the content flow works.

### 7.3 Community loops

- **Play 3 seasons, get a perk** (a paddle discount or exclusive tee). Light version of Volo Pass. [Comp: Volo Pass] [Comp: takeaway 8]
- **End-of-season socials**, filmed. [Comp: Honcho end-of-season social]
- **Venue co-posts and listings** on partner venue channels (Chicken N Pickle, Mile Hi Pickleball, Mega Courts). [Comp: Honcho partner-facility model] [Audit: venue names]
- **Community calendars**: submit every league start to local Pride and LGBTQ event calendars. [Comp: Las Vegas Pride, LGBTQ Colorado]
- **UGC reposting**: weekly "Pickle of the Week" from player tags. [Comp: Volo organic review content] [Inference]
